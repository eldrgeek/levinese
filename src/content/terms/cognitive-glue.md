A mechanism that lets competent subunits share stress, reward, memory, and control well enough to become a larger cognitive system. In the 2023 bioelectricity framing, the glue is the ion-channel and gap-junction network that binds cells into tissues with larger goals than any one cell can pursue. In the 2024 stress-sharing framing, the same functional role can be played by any channel that makes one unit's problem visible to others.
    <br><br>
    "Glue" is deliberately functional rather than material. Bioelectricity is the key biological example, but the deeper claim is about scale: a collective intelligence appears when local agents stop acting as isolated homeostats and begin to participate in a shared model of what matters. Cognitive glue expands the light cone of the whole by coupling the concerns of its parts.
